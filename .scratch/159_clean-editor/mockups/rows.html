R1 — Same columns, no strips

Grout / mortar move up into the product cell as small tags. Empty rows lose the “+ Extras” strip (a “+” shows on hover). Notes stay as one line. The search row gets thinner and loses its box.

R2 — Two-line product, fewer columns

SKU, coverage and extras move to a quiet second line under the product name. The SKU and Cov. columns go away, so the name gets the room. No strips under rows.

R3 — Ledger (densest)

One slim line per product, like a spreadsheet. Extras are tiny G / M letters beside the name, a note is a small icon. The add-a-product search lives in the area bar (＋ Add), not its own row.

R4 — Extras as their own lines

Not the most compact — the clearest. Grout and mortar are indented lines under the tile, with their own order and price, the way the printed estimate reads. Empty rows show nothing extra.
