(the split covers KERDI-BOARD / benches / niches / fasteners / KERDI-BAND /
DITRA / KERDI membranes — the **KERDI-SHOWER tray/kit/curb chapter and the

## Kit-contents facts the engine should encode

- **KERDI-BOARD-SC curb includes 2 inside + 2 outside KERECK waterproofing
  corners** (p.121) — a curbed build needs fewer standalone corner packs.
- **KERDI-BOARD-SN niche is self-contained** (p.122): niche + KERDI-BAND
  waterproofing frame + 8 ZS screws + 8 ZT washers in the box — a niche
  add-on brings no companion lines.
- KERDI-BOARD-SB benches: 16"×16" and 24"×24" triangular, 11-1/2"×38" and
  16"×48" rectangular, all 20" high, sloped tops (p.121).
- KERDI-BOARD thickness lineup 3/16"→2"; 48-wide panels in 32/64/96/120"
  lengths; ships by pallet/bundle from Schluter (the shop breaks bulk —
  their stock sheet is the sell-by-each truth).
- KERDI-BAND: 5" / 7-1/4" / 10" widths; 16'5" / 33' / 98'5" / 984' rolls (p.126).
- KERDI membrane: 8-mil; rolls 54/75/108/215/323 sf at 3'3" wide + WIDE
  6'7" rolls (323/646 sf — "covers wall floor to shower head height, fewer
  seams"); KERDI-DS is the 20-mil steam-room variant (p.130).
