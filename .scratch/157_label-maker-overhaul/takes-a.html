Picking two sizes from the stock search

Each result gets a checkbox. Clicking a name still fills the label straight away, as today. Tick two and the footer offers “One label, 2 sizes”: it turns on the second size and puts the bigger size first (by face area, so 24x48 beats 12x24), whatever order you ticked them in. Tick more than two and you get “Add N labels”, one label each (this replaces today's hidden shift-click).
