Project header — three layouts

Same job, same controls, three structures borrowed from how Shopify, Stripe, Linear and Zoho lay out a dense record header. Everything you can press in the real header is here; segments and chips are live so you can feel the click count. Throwaway — nothing in src/ changes.

D — Title band + property chips

Linear · Shopify

A thin title row (number, editable name, ERP, saved state, total) with the two primaries at the right and the housekeeping actions behind ⋯. Under it, one row of chips — every setting is a chip you click to open a small menu, exactly as Linear's Properties row and Shopify's order badges work. Notes are a single line that grows on focus. Trade-off: price level and estimate-shows become two clicks (chip → menu) instead of one; the header shrinks to ~100px.

E — Ruled grid, uniform rows

your idea, made to line up

Your hairline-grid instinct, but built so it can't mis-align: three rows of identical height, full-width horizontal rules, short inset vertical dividers (Stripe's trick — short lines never have to meet the lines in the row above). Every setting turns horizontal: Price level, Estimate shows and Waste become segmented controls on one line, so one click still does everything it does today. Row 1 is identity, row 2 is settings, row 3 is details. ~124px tall.

F — Sheet head

Stripe · Xero · Zoho

The header reads like the top of the estimate it prints: a quiet toolbar, then a hero (big name at left, big total at right, the way Stripe leads with the amount), then a facts strip — label-over-value cells for customer, builder, salesperson, level, shows, freight, waste. Each fact is a click to change (popover), like Zoho's quote strip. Most "modern" of the three, and the tallest at ~150px; settings are two clicks.

What the big products do

Mobbin · 2026-09-22

The recurring shape: a thin title row (identifier + name + status badges, primaries top-right, the rest behind a menu) and then one uniform strip — either facts (label-over-value) or interactive property chips. Nobody stacks vertical option lists in the header; choices open as menus or sit in horizontal segments, so every row is the same height and the lines line up for free.

Shopify — order page. "#1003 · Paid · Fulfilled" badges inline with the title, date line under, Refund / Return / Edit / Print / More actions top-right; everything else is cards below. screen
Stripe — payment / invoice. Leads with the amount and a status pill ("MYR 2.00 · Succeeded"), "Charged to ___" under it, Refund top-right; a Details column of label-over-value facts. screen · invoice
Linear — project overview. Title, summary, then a "Properties" row of chips (status, priority, lead, dates, team) each opening a menu. The most compact way to keep many settings one row high. screen
Zoho CRM — quote. A key-value strip (Quote Number / Stage / Owner / Valid Until / Carrier) directly under the title with Convert / Edit / ⋯ top-right. screen
Pipedrive — deal. Title with owner + follower pills, Won / Lost buttons top-right, then a full-width stage bar. screen
Attio — record. Title row with three text actions right; status chips under it; tab strip; facts in a right column. screen
