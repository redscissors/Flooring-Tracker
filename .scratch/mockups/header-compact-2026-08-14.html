3 · The ? pattern, app-wide

proposal

The ? becomes a tiny shared widget (HelpTip in widgets.jsx): a 14px circle that pops the full text on hover, keyboard focus, or tap — so it works on the shop iPads too, unlike the current title= tooltips. Try it: Like this. The popup is ink-on-paper, 240px wide, and can hold bold and multi-sentence rules without pushing the layout around.

The dividing line we'd follow: a ? hides standing rules — text that's true every day and only needs reading once. Text that reports state or a warning (amber import hazards, “ASSUMED” quantities, drift chips, empty states) stays inline, because hiding it hides the problem.
