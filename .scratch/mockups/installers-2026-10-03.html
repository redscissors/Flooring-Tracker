Installers

Every screen below is clickable, and they all share one set of sample data. Add an installer in Settings and they show up in the hammer picker. Add one to the job and they appear in the side box and on the printout. The companies are made-up examples.

General gets its own category list, just like Materials & add-ons does today. Pick a category on the left and its options open on the right. Styles holds Appearance and Project header, and Installers is new.

General

The hammer goes in the project's icon row, and its badge counts the installers on the job. The picker reads what's on the job (Tile, Hard Surface and Carpet here, because its vinyl and hardwood lines both count as Hard Surface). Installers who can do all of it come first, in green. Next come installers who can do part of it, sorted by how much they cover and then by priority. Clicking a row adds or removes that installer right away, so there's no Done button. On a screen too narrow for the box, the box is hidden and the hammer's badge count is the cue.

The installers print as the last block on the sheet, below the totals. One row per installer. The Trade column stacks the trades they're on for this job, one per line, so Goshen Floor Covering reads Tile / Hard Surface / Carpet. A trade they install that this job doesn't have is left off. Priority never prints, because that's internal.

Print block

Who sees the list

Installers are shared by the whole team, like the shop address, so one person adds a company and everyone can pick it.

What the job keeps

The job saves a copy of each installer's name and contact info when you add them. If you later edit or remove that installer in Settings, jobs you already quoted still print correctly.

Trades

Installers pick from three trades: Tile, Hard Surface and Carpet. Hard Surface covers hardwood, vinyl and laminate lines. Underlayment and Misc lines don't count as a trade on their own.
