Kits change later?
The project saves “2 Commercial” — not “2 Commercial + 2 Full”. If the job grows to 14 kits, Full re-figures to 6 on its own. Same idea as the shower swaps (ADR 0049: save the choice, not the part).
Typed both?
Then both numbers stick. If they cover fewer kits than the job needs, the line shows a short warning (like the qty-drift chip) instead of quietly under-ordering.
