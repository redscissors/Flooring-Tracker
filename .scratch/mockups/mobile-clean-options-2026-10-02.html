The double-tap bug

What happens: you open Price book from the bottom bar and tap a product. It fills the line, but then the search screen opens again with that SKU already typed, and you have to tap the product again or hit Cancel.

Why: the blank line you're adding to is drawn differently from a filled line. When the pick fills it, the row editor is thrown away and rebuilt, and the rebuilt editor still remembers “open in search mode” from the Price book tap. So the search opens a second time. It only happens through the bottom bar's Price book button, because that's the only way in that starts in search mode.

Fix: clear the “open in search” flag once a pick lands, and keep the line from being rebuilt, so one tap adds the product and drops you on its square-footage field. This is separate from the design work and doesn't depend on which options you pick.
