A2Cost beside the price, markups below

The price cell is the price box. Cost sits just left of it in the top row, and the caret starts there. Tab goes to the price and Tab again closes it and moves on down the row. A markup button sets the price and puts the caret on it, so Tab or Enter closes it. Enter closes from anywhere, and Esc or a click away closes it too. No margin line, no ✕, no hints.
