Price popup: grow out of the price cell

Click (or tab into) a price cell. The cell gets the ink line, and the cost & markup box grows out of it, the same way the search boxes now do. The cell stays live inside the box's top row, so you can still type the price there. Esc, Enter or a click elsewhere folds it back into the cell.

Speed:

AThe cell is the price field (recommended)

The popup's own Price box goes away; the price cell at the top of the box is where you type the price. The body is just Cost, Markup and the margin. No duplicate price, and the popup gets shorter. Tab walks cost → markup → back to the price cell.
