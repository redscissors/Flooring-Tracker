# Prototype outcome — selection-sheet print page (2026-09-08)

**Question:** the estimate print read like a quote or an order; what header
and framing make it read as a selection sheet — and does a body watermark
help?

**Method:** throwaway harness `.scratch/126_print-selection-sheet-protos/`
(kept as the record) rendering the REAL `EstimatePaper` body over the 090
fixture job with prototype mastheads swapped in above it, shot in screen and
print media. Five rounds: A (title-led) vs B (letterhead + stamp); three
takes on A (compact / letter / band); A with the fill watermark, labels
dropped, footer dropped; the tagline; the eyebrow.

**Answer (owner-picked, built into EstimatePrint.jsx the same day —
`.scratch/128_print-selection-sheet/`):**
- "FLOORING & TILE" eyebrow, big "SELECTION SHEET" title, tagline "Rough
  pricing and quantities for planning purposes only"; Keim mark right with
  N-number + date. The Rough Estimate badge is gone.
- People row keeps the names, drops the small-caps labels.
- Faint (6% black) diagonal SELECTIONS watermark on every page.
- No "Prepared with" footer.

**Rejected:** B's stamp; A2 letter-style prose rows and A3 title band;
outline watermark (owner chose fill); dropping the eyebrow entirely.
