3 · Compare tab

Compare shows sizes and drops brand words from lines

Today a Compare cell is just quantity + name + price, so "2× Schluter KERDI-BOARD 1/2" panel" twice in a row doesn't tell you which panels. What changes, in one place

The cleanup rules

Brand words come off item names

wedi® ™ Schluter KERDI- S-DRY Subliner Dry. Product names that tell two things apart stay: ALL-SET, PRO-SET, Building panel.

Size is its own column

In the build column and in every Compare cell. Read from the item's size on the price sheet. Blank when the sheet gives none.

One size format on both kit lists

Bold nominal in feet, then inches in grey, smallest first: 3′2″ × 5′ 38 × 60.

Standing help goes behind a ?

"Every price below is the full kit…" and "click one — the build column fills…" become ? tips on the heading, per ADR 0045. Warnings stay inline.

Same stock signal on both

Moss dot means stocked. Grey text with a hollow dot means special order. The Schluter "stock" pill goes.

Display only

Nothing about pricing, the solver or what's stored changes. This is how rows read inside the two configurators.

Decided 2026-09-28

Your answers

  1. Brand words come off names inside the configurators only. Lines added to the job, the print and order entry keep the full name ("Schluter KERDI-BAND 5" seam band").
  2. Kit-list sizes use wedi's form: bold feet, then inches in grey (3′2″ × 5′ 38 × 60).
  3. SKU is hidden on the kit list. Hovering a row shows it; the build column still prints it on the grey line.
